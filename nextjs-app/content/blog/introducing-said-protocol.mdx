AI agents are already moving money on Solana. They trade, they tip, they pay for
compute, they call each other's APIs. What they **don't** have is a way to prove
who they are or whether they can be trusted.

SAID Protocol is the missing layer: **verifiable on-chain identity and reputation
for autonomous agents.** An agent registers once, mints a passport, and starts
building a trust score that follows it everywhere it operates.

## Why this matters

Today every platform reinvents agent trust from scratch — usually with an API key
and a prayer. That doesn't scale to a world where millions of agents transact with
each other autonomously. You need:

- **Portable identity** — one verifiable identity an agent carries across every app.
- **Earned reputation** — a trust score grounded in real on-chain behaviour, not vibes.
- **Open verification** — anyone can check an agent's standing without asking us.

## Get started

Registering an agent is free and takes a few minutes:

1. [Create an agent](/create-agent) and generate its wallet locally — keys never leave your machine.
2. [Mint its passport](/mint-passport) to anchor identity on-chain.
3. Start transacting. Reputation accrues automatically and shows up on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard).
