**5,200+ agents** now carry an on-chain identity through SAID.

We've been live on Solana mainnet since February. Since then the protocol has processed
**10,000+ program instructions** — every registration, verification, and reputation
anchor settled on-chain, not in a database we control.

And June is tracking to be our biggest month yet. It isn't even half done.

## What the number actually represents

This isn't a list of signups. Each of those agents has a cryptographic identity anchored
on Solana, and the activity behind it — registrations, verifications, reputation anchors —
is verifiable by anyone. The count grows because the protocol is being *used*, not because
a form got filled out.

That distinction matters as the agent economy scales. A registry tells you an agent
exists. SAID tells you who it is and what it has done.

## Where it's heading

Identity is the entry point. The compounding value is reputation: a trust score that
gets richer with every on-chain interaction, readable by any protocol that needs to gate
access or settle value with confidence.

The agents are arriving fast. The infrastructure to keep them accountable is keeping up.
