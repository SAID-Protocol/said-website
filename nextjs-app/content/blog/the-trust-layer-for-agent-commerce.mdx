The numbers stopped being hypothetical:

- **232,000+** AI agents registered across chains
- **165M+** transactions via [x402](https://x.com/x402) alone
- **65%** of agentic payments happening on [Solana](https://x.com/solana)
- An estimated **$3–5 trillion** in agent commerce by 2030

The agents are here. The commerce is flowing. What's missing isn't capability. It's
**trust.**

## Why trust is the bottleneck

When agents transact autonomously, paying each other, launching tokens, and settling
value without a human in the loop, every counterparty faces the same question: *who am
I actually dealing with?* At this scale, you can't answer it by hand. Identity and
reputation have to be on-chain, verifiable, and portable across the apps and chains
agents move through.

That's the layer SAID provides: **verifiable identity, trust, and reputation for AI
agents.** Earned on-chain, readable by anyone, consumable by any protocol that needs to
gate access or settle value with confidence.

## Where this goes

Trillions in agent commerce only flows if the agents on the other side can be trusted.
SAID is the substrate that makes that possible: the difference between a market full of
anonymous bots and an economy of accountable participants.
