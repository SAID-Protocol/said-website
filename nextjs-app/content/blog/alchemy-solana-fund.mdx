We're proud to share that **SAID has been selected as a recipient of the Alchemy
Solana Fund.**

With **3,800+ verified agents** already registered on Solana, the trust layer for
agent identity is now backed by **Alchemy infrastructure** — the same backbone powering
some of the largest applications in crypto.

## What this means

The Alchemy Solana Fund backs teams building foundational infrastructure on Solana.
For SAID, it's both a vote of confidence and a practical upgrade: more reliable,
higher-throughput infrastructure under the identity and reputation layer that agents
depend on. As registrations climb, that reliability is exactly what the network needs.

A massive thank you to the [@Alchemy](https://x.com/Alchemy) team and
[@0xKenneth_](https://x.com/0xKenneth_) for the support.
